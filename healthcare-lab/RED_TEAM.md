# RED_TEAM.md — Adversarial gate

Runs **before** any final conclusion and **again** after the innovation concept. It is
not cosmetic. If it finds a material flaw, it must either lower confidence or change
the conclusion. A Red-Team section that changed nothing is a failed Red Team.

## The interrogation (answer every one, in writing)

**Against the evidence conclusion:**
1. What evidence contradicts this conclusion? (name it from the ledger)
2. What study, if it existed/were found, would make this conclusion wrong?
3. Are we cherry-picking supportive studies? Did we search for disconfirming ones?
4. Is there publication bias? (small positive trials, industry funding, unregistered)
5. Is there confounding? (observational data dressed as causal)
6. Is the sample representative of the population we care about?
7. Can this evidence be generalized — to other settings, and to Saudi Arabia?
8. Is the effect **clinically** meaningful, or only statistically significant?
9. Is the evidence current, or superseded?
10. Are there higher-quality studies we skipped?
11. Are Saudi conditions materially different (population, system, workforce, data)?
12. Are we reporting a surrogate outcome as if it were patient-important?
13. Are we confusing efficacy (trial) with effectiveness (real world)?

**Against the innovation concept (second pass):**
14. Is this novelty without value? What unmet need does it actually close?
15. What is the strongest reason this product fails? (adoption, workflow, reimbursement, regulation)
16. Who loses if this succeeds, and will they block it?
17. Is the "gap" real, or just unsearched? Does an existing solution already cover it?
18. Does the MVP actually test the riskiest assumption, or a safe one?
19. What evidence would a skeptical investor/regulator demand that we don't have?

## Output: Red-Team Findings
For each material finding:
- **Finding** — the flaw, concretely.
- **Impact** — what it threatens.
- **Action taken** — confidence downgraded (from→to) / conclusion changed / scope
  narrowed / flagged as open question. "None" is only acceptable with a reason.

## Discipline
- Steelman the opposite conclusion before dismissing it.
- "No contradicting evidence found" is only valid if you actually searched for it —
  say what search you ran.
- The Red Team is allowed to send the investigation back to Role A (new search) or
  Role B (re-appraisal). Use that power.
